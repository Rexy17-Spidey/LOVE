## Photographic Ethics & Preservation Rules
1. **Zero AI Alterations**: No facial features, eyes, nose, or lips have been morphed, reconstructed or replaced.
2. **Original Textures**: Authentic skin textures and natural lighting were strictly maintained.
3. **Preserved Insets**: Video-call screenshots retain both Bhavya and Rahul's insets completely.
4. **Resilient Fallbacks**: Every photograph includes an inline vector SVG fallback to ensure zero broken-image glyphs in any network condition.
