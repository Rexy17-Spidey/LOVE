# The Art of Her — selected photo inbox

This folder contains selected original photo uploads copied without edits. It is prepared for local integration into the website.

**Important:** These are source copies. Keep them unchanged. Create edited/optimised derivatives in separate folders; never overwrite these files. Do not reshape, retouch, regenerate or otherwise alter facial features.

## Privacy and selection

Selected video-call screenshots are now included separately in `video-call-memories/`. They were kept separate from the main portrait collection because they contain call interfaces. Do not publish raw call screenshots or UI captures without cropping interface details and checking for private information. One screenshot displaying a phone number was deliberately excluded. Several near-duplicates were also excluded.

The supplied side-by-side couple portrait is included as the primary image for the “Beside You” / promise section. Do not substitute a call screenshot.

## Editing requirements for the website

- Preserve originals exactly. Do not rename or overwrite these source files after importing.
- Make web-optimised copies (WebP or JPEG) only in a separate `assets/images/optimized/` directory.
- Use CSS `object-fit` and per-image focal positions for layout; do not destructively crop the source.
- No face reshaping, eye/nose/lip changes, expression changes, skin smoothing, AI beautification, face restoration or generated replacement features.
- Use only mild exposure, white balance, contrast and colour adjustments when needed.
- Keep the black-and-white portrait naturally monochrome.
- Keep the side-by-side couple image side-by-side, with both original portraits visible.
- Do not imply any supplied portrait was taken at the staircase, burger spot, canteen or confession unless the user confirms that.
- Add descriptive alt text that describes the visible image without inventing story details.
- Ensure all local paths are relative and GitHub Pages compatible.
